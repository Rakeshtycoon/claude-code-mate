## 1. In one line

**DiamCalc** is OctoNus Software's (Moscow) **diamond cut modeling + physically-based ray-tracing
simulation** program. From a polished diamond's 3D model it produces **photorealistic images,
ASET / Ideal-Scope / Hearts & Arrows views, and brilliance/fire/scintillation metrics**, and lets
a designer **create new cuts and optimize proportions**. It is the "design lab" tool behind the
practice of tuning facet angles to maximize sparkle.

## 2. Maker + history

- **Developer:** OctoNus Software Ltd., Moscow, Russia. "3DCALC" family of three: **DiamCalc**
  (diamonds), **GemCalc** (colored gems), **GemAdviser** (free viewer). (legacy.octonus.com; zoominfo)
- **Scientific roots — Moscow State University (MSU) Gemological Center** (since 1995). Key people:
  **Sergey Sivovolenko** (OctoNus CEO), **Yuri Shelementiev** (MSU lab head), **Roman Serov**;
  decade-long collaboration including Garry Holloway (HCA). (octonus.com/company; ResearchGate 263843604)
- **Lexus connection:** Surat (India) Lexus group (~2002) distributes/co-markets DiamCalc &
  DiamCalc Pro (lexusindia.in) — hence the "Lexus Soft" name. (issuu; lexusindia.in)
- **Version timeline:** 3.0 (introduced HDR movies/panoramas) → 3.2 beta (manual 7 July 2009) →
  **3.3 latest; build 3.3.53.724, 13 April 2017.** (pressrelease_dc3; software.informer)
- ⚠️ OctoNus's exact founding year is not firmly documented (mid-1990s inferred).

## 3. Editions

| Edition | What it does |
|---|---|
| **DiamCalc (Standard)** | Weight calc, proportion analysis, 3D + photorealistic rendering, cut grading, reports |
| **DiamCalc Pro** | All of the above **+ optimization sweeps** — vary any 1–2 proportions over a From–To–Step range and chart each combo's quality metrics. For designing new cuts and optimizing existing proportions. (~10× Standard price — dated figure) |
| **DiamCalc Color** | Specialized to optimize proportions for the best fancy **color** |
| **GemAdviser** | **Free, read-only** `.GEM` viewer (effectively the demo). A time-limited DiamCalc demo also exists |

- ⚠️ "DiamCalc 3D" is not a separate edition; **Cut Designer** is a module inside DiamCalc.
  (pressrelease_dc3; lexusindia.in/so-dc-pro; octonus.com/3dcalc)

**Pricing/specs:** Standard ~**$308–395** (dated; verify on live store); Windows, legacy specs
(Pentium-class, 128 MB–1 GB RAM). (octonus store snippet; gemologyonline)

## 4. Ray-tracing engine

- **Single-ray trace mode:** shows one ray's entry → internal reflections → exit (e.g. Asscher ~2
  internal reflections vs. oval ~7). Pedagogical. (legacy.octonus .../11.phtml)
- **Full physically-based rendering:** models light absorption + multiple internal reflections +
  dispersion — the basis for simulating brilliance and fire. (octonus.com/3dcalc)
- **HDR imaging (v3.0+):** reproduces the "flare/bloom" of bright sparkles (rendered larger than
  the facet, extending past the edge) — a scintillation factor ordinary ray-tracers miss.
  (pressrelease_dc3)
- Input: a scanned real 3D model (actual facet positions + symmetry deviations) **or** a
  parametric cut. Modes: refraction/outline, stereo (3D), tilt/movie. (octonus.com/3dcalc)

## 5. Virtual optical scopes (built-in)

- **ASET**, **Ideal-Scope**, **Hearts & Arrows** — all generated by ray-tracing the 3D model.
  (octonus.com/3dcalc; beyond4cs)
- ASET color map: **red** = high-angle intense return, **green** = lower-angle/indirect (leakage
  hint), **blue** = obstruction/contrast. (yourdiamondguru)
- **HDR panorama lighting environments** (ballroom/forest/hillside radiance maps) for previewing in
  real-world surroundings. **Movie/panorama/tilt** promotional videos. (hdrpanorama; hdrdemomovie)
- ⚠️ Exact preset names ("Office/Diamond") not fully confirmed.

## 6. Light-performance metrics

- OctoNus/MSU defined working metrics for **brilliance, fire, scintillation** — the basis of
  beauty-assessment models. (octonus.com/3dcalc; ResearchGate)
- Can simulate the GIA/AGS framework: **WLR** (Weighted Light Return ≈ brilliance), **DCLR**
  (Dispersed Colored Light Return ≈ fire — GIA computed over 26,000+ combos). (gia G&G Fall 2001)
- Produces **AGS ASET grade-map style** output; DiamCalc is an accepted producer of ASET images.
  (niceice / AGS ASET)
- ⚠️ Discrete numeric "WLR/contrast" UI readouts not confirmed (implied by the literature).

## 7. Cut-design & optimization workflow (the key part)

**Facet editing:**
- Parameter panel has two tabs — **"Proportions"** and **"Advanced"**. Advanced exposes per-facet
  values: Crown angle (**min/max/avg**), Pavilion angle (**min/max/avg**), Star facets, Lower
  facets, Girdle. (doc_advparams.phtml)
- Each facet's geometry is **slope (angle/depth) + azimuth (twist/index)** — the same index
  manipulation used in painting/digging. (helium/parameter18; doc_cut_import)
- **Cut Designer** builds variable/parametric models; you can trace a **background photo** to
  reconstruct a wireframe. (diamcalc_32; gemologyonline)

**Optimization (Pro):**
- **"Vary parameter / Keep fixed"** — sweep any 1–2 proportions over **From–To–Step** → auto-
  generate a model series → metrics for each. (lexusindia.in/so-dc-pro)
- Output: text reports → 2D/3D **optimization charts** + HTML tables of rendered images. From
  **10+ light-performance responses**, generate ASET charts for **thousands of proportion combos**
  to find the combination maximizing **brilliance/fire/scintillation**. (lexusindia.in/so-dc-pro)

> This directly serves the goal of systematically varying crown/pavilion/star/lower-half angles —
> keeping averages in the ideal range — to find the **maximum-light** facet combination.

**Weight vs light trade-off:** models rough planning — more cut polish = more weight loss —
to find the best balance. (gemologyonline; pricescope wiki)

**Concrete steps:**
1. Start from a template (Tolkowsky/fancy) **or** import a scanned stone (`.srn`/`.stl`/Helium).
2. Edit facets in Proportions/Advanced/Cut Designer — adjust slope + azimuth per facet/layer.
3. Build a parametric "variable" model.
4. **(Pro)** run an optimization sweep — From/To/Step → charts + ASET tables.
5. Appraise with photoreal + ASET/Ideal-Scope/H&A + GIA/HRD/AGA/TU grading; pick the combo
   balancing brilliance/fire/scintillation against weight recovery.

## 8. Technical — formats, scanner integration

- **Native formats:** `.dmc` (cut definition), `.gem` (read-only GemAdviser viewer).
  (doc_cut_import; ideal-scope)
- **Import:** **GemCad `.asc`, AutoCAD, Sarin `.srn`, OGI/GIA `.stl`, Helium, PACOR `.mmd`**
  (non-convex facets triangulated). (doc_cut_import; srn_stl FAQ)
- **Export:** `.gem`/`.dmc`, **binary + ASCII STL** (batch STL), **AVI movie + QuickTime**.
  (diamcalc_2-8; _2-2-0)
- **Scanner pipeline:** OctoNus **Helium Polish/Rough → DiamCalc** (primary). Per-facet
  **inclination + azimuth** capture, including extra facets and conical-girdle distortions.
  **Sarin/OGI/GIA** scans import. (helium-polish; doc_cut_import)
- **Proportion set:** table %, crown angle, pavilion angle, star %, lower-half %, girdle, culet,
  crown/pavilion/total depth + MIC, roundness, Kozibe, fish-eye, spread. (gia modeling PDF; helium-polish)
- **Cut-grade standards mapped:** **GIA, HRD, AGA, Russian TU** (newer OctoNus tools — HP
  Carbon/Oxygen — integrate GIA-approved **Facetware**). ⚠️ "**AGS**" specifically not confirmed —
  the confirmed module is **AGA**. OctoNus notes its GIA-grading is "not official GIA data."
  (appr GIA/AGA pages; giafacetware)

## 9. Users + research adoption

- **Users:** gemologists, appraisers, labs, cutters, manufacturers; vendors — **Whiteflash**
  (DiamCalc education page), super-ideal/H&A ecosystem (Brian Gavin, Good Old Gold). (whiteflash; ideal-scope)
- **Research:** MSU Gemological Center; ResearchGate paper on brilliance/scintillation/fire uses
  "Modern Tolkowsky" DiamCalc models; fancy-color project. (ResearchGate 263843604; octonus projects)
- ⚠️ GIA's own G&G cut modeling (Hemphill/Reinitz 1998/2001) used **GIA's internal** ray-tracer,
  not DiamCalc. DiamCalc is a **parallel/independent** industry + MSU tool. (gia G&G)
- **Use cases:** optical-symmetry/H&A checks, new-cut design, appraising/recut "before-after,"
  marketing imagery, education.

## 10. DiamCalc vs alternatives

| Tool | Role |
|---|---|
| **DiamCalc** | Design + optical simulation + grade prediction (the analysis brain) |
| **Sarine** DiaMension/Advisor | Production scanning hardware + rough yield planning |
| **OGI / GemCad** | Scanning / faceting-CAD (interoperate via `.asc`/`.stl`) |
| **AGS ASET / GIA Facetware** | Official light-performance / cut grade — DiamCalc makes compatible images, not the official grade |

> Sarine = "measure the real stone + plan rough." DiamCalc = "design/model/simulate + optimize
> light." Complementary; DiamCalc imports Sarin scans (despite OctoNus vs Sarine patent disputes).
> (sarine.com; octonus open-letter; legacy doc_cut_import)

## 11. Limitations / criticisms (forum-sourced)

- **Garbage-in/garbage-out:** vendors rarely supply minor-facet data, so the model assumes average
  angles and "perfect wireframe symmetry" that may not match the real stone. (pricescope 18120; 243860)
- **Data-access barrier:** modeling a specific stone needs the vendor's `.stl/.srn` scan — many
  won't supply it. (pricescope 243860)
- **Simulated scopes "too clean":** DiamCalc renders crisp facets while real ASET/Ideal-Scope
  photos blur — so it can flatter "mushy" facets (marquise/fancy). Accuracy depends on scan
  resolution. Practitioners still report "remarkable agreement" with real ASETs.
  (prosumerdiamonds; pricescope 205766)

## 12. Bottom line

DiamCalc is a **physically-based diamond ray-tracer + parametric cut designer** with genuine
scientific provenance (MSU; parallels GIA's G&G modeling; underpins AGS ASET-style analysis). Its
most relevant capability is **DiamCalc Pro's optimization sweep**: systematically vary
crown/pavilion/star/lower-half angles — keeping averages in the ideal range — to find the
combination giving maximum brilliance/fire/scintillation, then verify with ASET/Ideal-Scope. Main
caveat: simulation is only as good as the input scan, and simulated scopes look "cleaner" than
real optics.
