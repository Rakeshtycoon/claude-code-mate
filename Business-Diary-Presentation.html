The Problem

The limits of a paper diary

  • You write every day, but data gets lost — diaries are forgotten, torn or misplaced.
  • Hard to compare with last month or last year.
  • Sales, Collection, Goals, Expenses — all in separate places.
  • No graphs or summary — you never see the big picture.
The Solution

One place for your complete business diary

PA Business Diary is the digital version of your paper diary — the same structure, but with automatic totals, graphs and backup.

Monthly & Yearly Plan

Set the target once, the rest is automatic

  • Yearly Plan — set each month's sales target at the start of the year.
  • That target shows automatically in Monthly Plan and Daily.
  • Achieved — calculated automatically from the daily entries.
  • Last Year — same month a year ago, auto-filled; Growth % in green/red.
